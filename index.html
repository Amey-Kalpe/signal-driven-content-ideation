Governance readiness is becoming the real AI advantage

For many institutional teams, the question is no longer whether AI can produce a plausible answer. It is whether that answer can move through a workflow that people trust.

Recent client conversations point to a consistent tension: teams want to move faster, but accountability, evidence and review are still being designed after the experiment begins. CMD-104 - ED-28

A more durable approach begins with the decision chain. Sources are approved before use, recommendations remain linked to evidence, and people approve the moments that carry editorial or compliance risk. EMD-17

This turns governance from a final checkpoint into an operating capability: one that supports faster, more confident adoption rather than slowing it down.
